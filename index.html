Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add multiple dogs to my PawFolio account?

Navigate to "My Dogs" from the top navigation bar and click "+ Register New Dog". You can switch active profiles anytime using the dropdown menu on your Dashboard.

How does the Emergency QR Vault work?

The Emergency Vault creates a simplified digital record showing critical allergies, blood type, microchip number, and primary vet phone contacts for emergency situation access.

Can I export vaccination certificates as PDFs?

Yes! In the "Vaccinations" section, each completed vaccination log has an official PDF download button suitable for dog boarding, grooming, or travel requirements.
